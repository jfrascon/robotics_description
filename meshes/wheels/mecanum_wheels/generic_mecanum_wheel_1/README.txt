The left/right mecanum wheels represented in the files
generic_mecanum_wheel_1_left.dae
generic_mecanum_wheel_1_left_low_res.stl
generic_mecanum_wheel_1_right.dae
generic_mecanum_wheel_1_right_low_res.stl

have dimensions of:
radius = 0.127 m
thickness = 0.1 m

and the mass of these wheels is 6.5 kg (value obtained from the rbkairos robot from Robotnik)

If you need to scale the mesh for a project, be aware of scaling the mass, radius and thickness
properly.
